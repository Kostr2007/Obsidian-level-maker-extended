## Four coherent hypotheses

These are **project hex hypotheses**, not sampled Apple colors or mandatory Apple values. B/C test temperature at similarly faint strength; D tests a more visible neutral plane. All fills are opaque.

| Option / exact light fill | Hypothesis and reference | Expected benefit | Cost to inspect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **A — current white `#ffffff`** | Preserve the current continuous white editor/help field and existing thin divider. It remains the control and a valid final choice. | Maximum lightness and existing contrast against the eight pale gallery tiles; least surface subdivision. | Help and editing rely primarily on their boundary, headings and position to communicate different roles. |
| **B — very soft cool neutral `#f7f8fa`** | A faint cool veil separates help from the white editor. Interpret the quiet supplementary panes in Finder Gallery / Notes at lower strength. | Small role distinction that relates to Obsidian's existing cool neutral chrome without adding a new component. | May be too faint to justify the choice; fixed `#f1f1f4` Ammo tiles become less distinct than on white. |
| **C — soft warm neutral `#faf8f4`** | The same low-strength separation with a warm temperature, rather than a denser pane. Finder/Notes support separation of roles; this warmth is our own hypothesis. | A softer reading surface for descriptive help, while retaining the editor's white field. | Warm/cool adjacency may look tinted beside the established sidebar and tiles; small colored sprite details and captions need inspection. |
| **D — stronger neutral `#e7e7eb`** | Make the supplementary plane more explicit, in the cool gray family of the existing `#eeeeef` sidebar. Finder Gallery is the strongest composition reference. | Clearest separation from the white editor; the eight unchanged Ammo tiles become individually lighter islands on the pane. | Greatest risk of a heavy third column and weaker tile boundaries. Exact sidebar `#eeeeef` was deliberately avoided because it is almost the same color as `#f1f1f4` tiles. D is slightly deeper so their rounded silhouettes have a chance to remain visible; screenshots must decide. |
