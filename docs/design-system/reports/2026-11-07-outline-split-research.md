## Visual provenance and platform cautions

The [catalog manifest](../references/apple/manifest.json) records exact URLs, SHA-256, temporary inspection paths and source-specific limitations for MAC-18–23. No external imagery was added to the repository.

- Outline intro is a **stylized red diagram**, with four columns, disclosure triangles, indented child images and full-row selection. It demonstrates structure, not Finder colors, production pixels or a required palette.
- Split intro is a **stylized sidebar/canvas/inspector diagram**; three additional macOS laptop diagrams show side-by-side, stacked and combined panes. Their proportions are illustrations. The article's one-third/two-thirds layout guidance belongs to **tvOS**, not macOS; title-placement rules in the following visionOS subsection are not general macOS prescriptions.
- The complete outline and split articles, including abstracts, all best practices, all platform subsections and split change log, were read through their [outline DocC payload](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/design/human-interface-guidelines/outline-views.json) and [split DocC payload](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/design/human-interface-guidelines/split-views.json). Split's latest listed change is June 9, 2025 (iOS/iPadOS considerations); this is not a screenshot build date. Slider body, platform sections and change log were also read via its [payload](https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/data/design/human-interface-guidelines/sliders.json).
- HIG image references starting `/images/com.apple.HIG/` resolve here under **`https://developer.apple.com/tutorials/images/com.apple.HIG/`**. Resolving the outline intro at the domain root or under `/design/human-interface-guidelines/` returned 404; the matching `docs-assets` path returned 403. The `/tutorials/` URL returned 200 and the actual image was opened. This also gives a retrieval strategy for earlier catalog research that could not resolve relative assets; it does not retroactively claim those images were viewed.
- Slider specimens show a rounded, circular-looking white thumb, while current macOS prose calls the linear thumb a narrow lozenge. Treat exact contour as context/version dependent; neither establishes the required Obsidian contour. The Energy Saver crop's actual OS build is unknown and its historical setting name must not be presented as today's complete System Settings design.
- Current unversioned Finder and Settings guides identify **macOS 27 Golden Gate** in page metadata. Versioned Appearance guides identify **macOS Sequoia 15** and **macOS Tahoe 26**. This establishes guide context, not independent verification of artwork capture/build. `MAC-14`, `MAC-22` and `MAC-23` are catalog IDs, not OS version numbers.
- Text, icons and page content were checked on Desktop & Dock, display brightness, display settings and sound output guides. They did not supply a whole-window Settings slider image. That specific evidence remains **UNVERIFIED**; HIG slider illustrations and the explicitly identified Energy Saver crop are the selected slider visual evidence.

## Proposed acceptance criteria, if a user selects an experiment

1. **Category tiles:** retain current names, ordering, original glyph meaning and persistent selected row; use original backgrounds; selection and keyboard focus remain recognizable without color alone. Compare matched light/dark, minimum-width and large-text captures. Do not revisit ART-01/02/03 as part of this scope.
2. **Slider finish:** retain range, exact value entry, focus, keyboard adjustment, pointer behavior and CONFIG semantics; increase thumb/track distinction only if the paired screenshots support it. Ticks need meaningful values, not decoration. Linux Qt hit area and logical scaling are project decisions; verify them independently of the visual track width.
3. **Light inspector surface:** test a quiet, opaque surface distinction while preserving accepted content order and widths. Keep primary content dominant; verify readable secondary text, controls and focus in both appearances. No new draggable divider or pane system is implied.
